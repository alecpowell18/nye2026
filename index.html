FAQs
What is the dress code?

Black tie optional. We’d love for everyone to dress up with us! Tuxedos and gowns are welcome, but suits and cocktail or formal dresses are perfect too.

Is this a wedding?

Not really!

Will the celebration be indoors or outdoors?

The celebration will take place primarily indoors, with some outdoor spaces available throughout the evening. We recommend bringing a layer for the cooler coastal weather.

Can I bring a plus one?

Yes! Everyone is welcome to bring a plus one. Please be sure to include your guest’s name when you RSVP.

Are children invited?

Of course! Please include them when you RSVP.

Will dinner be served?

Yes! Dinner, drinks, and plenty of late-night celebrating will be provided.

Is there parking or valet?

Yes, valet parking is available at the resort for $18 per vehicle for guests attending the event.

Overnight guests: $60 per night, including in-and-out privileges.

Where should I stay?

We have reserved a room block at Alila Marea Beach Resort at a rate of $349+ per night, with a two-night minimum stay. Please note that the room rate does not include the 10% room tax, $27 nightly resort fee, or $60 nightly overnight parking fee. Book your room here.

Do you have a registry?

No!

What time will the reception end?

The celebration will officially wrap up at 12:30 AM, after we ring in 2027 together!
